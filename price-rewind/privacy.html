Price Rewind: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-24 · Plantburgh Products LLC, a Pennsylvania company owned by Plantburgh LLC

The short version: Price Rewind works out what a thing is right on your phone. Your photos, tags and notes stay on your phone. There is no account. The free plan shows our own cards and may show Google ads; Pro shows no ads.

Who we are

Price Rewind is made by Plantburgh Products LLC, a Pennsylvania company owned by Plantburgh LLC. Questions: contact@plantburgh.com.

What the app keeps

Your tags, the photos you snap, boards, time capsules, tag colors and settings. They are stored only on this phone, in the app's private storage. There is no account and we never receive them.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only while the Snap screen is open. Working out what an item is (image labels, barcodes and, on phones that have it, Google's on-device Gemini Nano) runs on the phone. Photos are never uploaded to identify an item.

Prices and facts

Prices and facts come from data files the app downloads from our website (GitHub Pages) and keeps on the phone. That download carries what any web request does (your IP address and device type) and nothing about your tags or photos.

Our own cards

On the free plan the app downloads a short list of our own apps and partner deals from our server to show as cards. The request names the app, not you.

Ads on the free plan

When ads are turned on, the free plan shows a Google AdMob banner. Google may use your phone's advertising ID, IP address and device details to show and measure ads under Google's policies, and asks for consent where the law requires it. Your photos and tags are never sent to advertisers. Pro shows no ads.

eBay links

"What's it worth" opens an eBay search for sold items in your browser. That link carries our eBay Partner Network tag, so eBay may pay us a small commission if you buy. eBay's own privacy policy covers what happens on eBay.

Purchases

Pro is billed by Google Play. Google tells the app which plan you have. We never see your payment details.

Notifications

Time capsule reminders are scheduled on the phone. Nothing is sent to us.

Sharing and backups

Data leaves the phone only when you share a card, export a spreadsheet, save a backup ZIP or email us about a fact, and you choose where it goes. If Android backup is on, Android may include your tags and some photos in your own Google account backup.

Deleting

Settings › Delete everything erases every tag, photo, capsule and setting on this phone. Uninstalling the app also removes it. We hold no copy.

Children

Price Rewind is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions: contact@plantburgh.com.
