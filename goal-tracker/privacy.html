Goal Tracker: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-10 · Plantburgh Products LLC, a Pennsylvania company owned by Plantburgh LLC

The short version: Your goals, check-ins, notes and reminders live on your phone. There is no Goal Tracker account and we run no server that stores your data. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your phone's advertising ID. If you turn on sync, your goals go to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive, not to us.

What the app keeps on your phone

Your goals, schedules, check-ins, counts, notes, pauses, reminders, widget settings and level/XP, in a database on the phone. Automatic backups (the last 7 days) are kept on the phone too.

Ads (free version)

The free version shows a banner from Google AdMob. To pick and measure ads, Google receives your device's advertising ID, IP address, rough location from that IP, and basic device and app information. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and several US states) the app asks for your consent first; if you say no, you get non-personalized ads. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone's settings. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Your goals, notes and check-ins are never sent to Google or to any advertiser.

When AdMob has no ad, the same space shows one of our own products or a partner offer (labelled "Sponsored"). The list of offers is downloaded from our website about once a day; that request, like any web request, shows our host (GitHub Pages) your IP address. Tapping an offer opens it in your browser. Partner links may let the partner (for example CJ Affiliate and the store behind it) know the visit came from us, so we can earn a commission. That partner's own privacy policy applies on their site.

Rewarded ads are only shown if you choose to watch one (for example, to earn a streak freeze).

Remove Ads purchase

The one-time Remove Ads purchase is handled by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your card or payment details; the store only tells the app that you own it. After you buy it, the app stops requesting ads.

Sync with Google Drive (optional, off by default)

If you turn on sync, you sign in with Google and allow Goal Tracker one narrow permission: a hidden app folder in your Google Drive. Your goals and history are saved there as one file and copied to your other phones. The app cannot see your other Drive files, and we never receive the file: it goes only between your phone and your Google account. Google's sign-in gives the app your email address so it can show "Syncing as …"; it stays on your phone.

Turn sync off any time in Settings. "Turn off and remove from Drive" also deletes the copy in your Drive. You can also remove Goal Tracker's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Notifications and the widget

Reminders are scheduled on the phone. On a locked screen they show only "GoalTracker", not your goal. The home-screen widget shows today's habits to anyone who can see your home screen; remove it or choose which habits it shows in Settings.

Backups and exports

Backup files you save or share go only where you send them. Android backup: if Google backup is on for your phone, Android may copy this app's data to your own Google account, encrypted with your screen lock. We never see that copy.

Feedback you choose to send

Send beta feedback shows you the full text first (your note, the app version and basic phone details) and copies it. It goes only where you paste it.

What we never do

We do not sell or rent your data. We run no analytics of our own and no server that stores your goals, so we cannot see them.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it; uninstalling the app removes it. The Drive copy, if you used sync, is removed with "Turn off and remove from Drive" or from your Drive's app data settings. Advertising data is handled by Google under its policy; you can reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Children

Goal Tracker is not directed to children under 13 and its ads are not set up for children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
