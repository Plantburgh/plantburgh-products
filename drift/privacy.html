Drift: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-20 · Plantburgh Products LLC, a Pennsylvania company owned by Plantburgh LLC

The short version: Drift saves your progress on your phone. It has no account, no ads and no internet connection of its own.

Who we are

Drift is made by Plantburgh Products LLC, a Pennsylvania company owned by Plantburgh LLC. Questions: contact@plantburgh.com.

What the game keeps

Your level progress, coins, upgrades and settings, stored only on this phone. We never receive them.

Internet, ads and tracking

Drift does not connect to the internet, shows no ads and has no analytics.

Backups

If Android backup is on, Android may include Drift's save in your own Google account backup.

Deleting

Clear Drift's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the game to erase your progress.

Children

Drift is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Contact

Questions: contact@plantburgh.com.
