1. What Replicost is, and what it is not

  • a quote, a bid, or an offer to perform work
  • an appraisal, a valuation, or an insurance settlement figure

Prices move, markets differ street by street, and no model of a job is the job. Treat every number as a starting point for a conversation with someone who will stand behind their figure. Do not make an irreversible financial decision on ours alone.

We cannot recover them, because we never had them. These flags tell you a rule exists. They do not tell you how it applies to you. They are drawn from data with a stated vintage, and rules change without telling us. Always confirm with the authority that actually decides — your municipality, your state board, your insurer, or your attorney — before relying on one.
