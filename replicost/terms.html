1. What Replicost is, and what it is not

Replicost computes estimates. It takes a subject, applies cost data we maintain, and returns a range with its sources and a stated confidence.

An estimate from Replicost is not:

  • a quote, a bid, or an offer to perform work
  • an appraisal, a valuation, or an insurance settlement figure
  • a construction, engineering, or design document
  • legal, financial, tax, or professional advice

Always check with a professional. Before you hire, buy or sign, have a licensed professional look at the job and get a few written quotes. Replicost helps you know what to expect and what to ask; it does not replace someone who has seen the work.

Prices are United States prices. Every figure comes from US sources and is worked out in US dollars for a US market. If you choose to see figures in another currency, they are converted at an exchange rate that ships with the app and carries the date it was read. A converted figure is not a local price for another country, and the rate may have moved.

A quote check is not an accusation. When you check a contractor's quote, Replicost spreads their number across the job in the way such jobs usually split, and uses any of their own figures you type in. That split is our inference unless the contractor confirms it. Ask them before you rely on it.

Prices move, markets differ street by street, and no model of a job is the job. Treat every number as a starting point for a conversation with someone who will stand behind their figure. Do not make an irreversible financial decision on ours alone.

These flags tell you a rule exists. They do not tell you how it applies to you. They are drawn from data with a stated vintage, and rules change without telling us. Always confirm with the authority that actually decides — your municipality, your state board, your insurer, or your attorney — before relying on one.
