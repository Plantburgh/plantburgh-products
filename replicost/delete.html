Replicost: Delete Your Data

Last updated: 2026-11-25 · Plantburgh Products LLC, a Pennsylvania company owned by Plantburgh LLC

Open Replicost and choose You › Erase everything on this device. That removes every saved estimate, project, ledger, report, note, your shop profile, the home-screen widget's copy of your last estimate and your stored Gemini key. Nothing is kept on a server. Photos identified on the phone (its built-in Gemini or labeler) never left it. Photos identified with your own Gemini key were sent to Google under that key; delete them from your Google account. Crash reports, if the app ever crashed, are kept by Google Firebase for 90 days and contain nothing you entered.
