Layover Logic Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-07-28 · revised 2026-11-21 · Plantburgh Products LLC, a Pennsylvania company owned by Plantburgh LLC

Layover Logic collects nothing. There is no account, no server run by us, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your phone. We cannot see your roster, your layovers, your base, your airline or anything you save.

No account, no server

There is nothing to sign in to. The app runs in your browser and holds no profile about you anywhere else. We run no backend that receives your data.

What stays on your device

Your profile (the name you type, a photo if you add one, your airline, your base, your role and your language), your layovers, imported rosters, hotels, settings, the colours you pick, your bedtime checklist, saved places, notes and ratings are kept in your browser’s storage on your own phone. If you clear your browser data or remove the app, that information is gone. We hold no copy to restore from.

Add Layover Logic to your home screen. iOS erases stored data for websites you have not opened in seven days, and exempts apps installed to the home screen. Crew often go longer than that between trips.

Your roster

Calendar files (.ics), roster PDFs and pasted text are read on your device. The file is never uploaded and never sent to us or to anyone else. The software that reads PDFs is served from our own site.

Photo import

If you photograph or screenshot a roster, the text recognition runs on your device. The image is never sent anywhere. The recognition engine and its language data download from our own site the first time you use the feature, and work with no connection after that.

Location

The app asks for your location only when you tap “Use my location” to pin your hotel or a place you add. The position is used once to place the pin and is stored on your device with it. It is not sent to us.

If you type a hotel address instead, the address you type is sent to OpenStreetMap’s search service (Nominatim) to find it on the map. That request goes from your phone to OpenStreetMap and carries the text you typed, not your name or your roster.

Map links

Tapping directions opens your phone’s maps app or Google Maps with the place’s address. What happens after that is covered by that app’s own policy.

Activity log

While the app is in beta it keeps a record of app opens, screens viewed, cities opened and errors, so crashes can be diagnosed. This record stays on your device and is never transmitted. It is removed when you delete your data.

What you choose to send

Feedback, a place you suggest, a hotel correction and your reason for leaving are sent only when you tap send, through your own mail or share sheet, and you see the exact text first. If you do not send, nothing leaves the device. What you send reaches contact@plantburgh.com and is used to fix the app and the guide.

If the app cannot read your schedule, or reads it wrong, you can send it to us so we can teach the app that layout. The schedule is attached only when the box for it is ticked. A schedule can carry your name or employee number, so untick the box if you do not want to send it.

Inviting crew

When you send the app to someone from inside it, the link carries a short tag made from your first name (for example “mara”), so the person who opens it can see who invited them. The tag is kept on their phone with their own activity log. It is not sent to us, and nothing about your schedule is in the link.

Calendar files and money

Putting tonight or your month on your phone’s calendar makes a small file on your phone that your calendar app opens. The exchange rate on the money screen is the one you type; the app looks nothing up.

Deleting your data

Me → Delete my data removes everything the app has stored on that phone: profile, schedule, hotels, notes, the activity log and the offline copy of the guide. Because we hold no copy, that is the whole deletion. There is nothing to request from us.

A backup file you saved, and calendar events you exported to your phone’s calendar, are yours and stay where you put them until you delete them.

No analytics, no tracking, no advertising

None. No page-view counters, no crash-reporting services, no advertising identifiers, no fingerprinting and no tracking cookies.

Hosting

The app’s files are served by Firebase Hosting (Google). Like any web host, it receives your IP address when your phone downloads the app or an update. We do not look up or keep those records.

Children

Layover Logic is for working flight crew and is not directed at children under 13. We collect no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised date above changes with it. We hold no contact details for you, so we cannot notify you directly. The current version is always in the app and at this address.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact@plantburgh.com.
