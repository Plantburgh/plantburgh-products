Swim safely

Workouts, plans and pace targets are general training guidance, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new exercise program. Swim where a lifeguard is on duty, never swim alone, never practise breath-holding (it can cause shallow water blackout), and stop if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath.
