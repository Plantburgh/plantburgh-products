Swim safely

Workouts, plans and pace targets are general training guidance, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new exercise program. Swim where a lifeguard is on duty, never swim alone, never practice breath-holding (it can cause shallow water blackout), and stop if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath. Read Swim safe in the app.

Swimming, open-water swimming and training carry real risks, including injury, drowning and death. You choose when, where and how hard you swim, and you are responsible for your own safety and for following lifeguards, posted rules and local conditions. Safety features can fail

They can fail or be late: a dead or switched-off phone, no signal, a full or muted phone, Android stopping or updating the app, a lost Bluetooth link, or GPS error. Never rely on them instead of a person who knows where you are. In an emergency call 911 or your local emergency number.
