Swimtime Pal: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026 · Plantburgh Products LLC, a Pennsylvania company owned by Plantburgh LLC

The short version: Your swims, plans and settings stay on your phone. There is no account and we run no server that stores them. The Free plan shows ads; Pro shows none. Location is used only during an open-water swim you start, and only while the app is in use.

Who we are

Swimtime Pal is made by Plantburgh Products LLC, Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. Questions: contact@plantburgh.com.

What Swimtime Pal keeps

Your swimmer details, workouts, training plan progress, pace tests, personal bests and settings. They are stored only on this phone, in Swimtime Pal's private storage. There is no account and we never receive your swimming.

Ads on Free

The Free plan shows ads. To show them, Swimtime Pal connects to the internet to fetch ad cards from our ad feed: small pills for partner deals and for our own apps. That request carries what any web request does (your IP address and the app name) and nothing about your swimming. Tapping a pill goes through our ad link, which counts the tap, then opens the partner's or our site in your browser; partner sites have their own privacy policies.

Google ads

When Google AdMob ads are switched on for Free, Google may use your phone's advertising ID to show and measure ads, under Google's policies. Where the law asks for it, we ask for your consent first. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Pro

Pro shows no ads and makes no internet requests of its own. Google Play handles your subscription.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Google Play. Google tells the app which Swimtime Pal plan your Google account has. We never see your payment details.

Backups

If Android backup is on, Android may include Swimtime Pal's data in your Google account backup, under your Google settings. A backup file you save from Settings is yours to keep wherever you choose.

Sharing

Your data leaves the phone only when you save a backup file, export a CSV or print a workout, and you choose where it goes; or when you let a phone assistant read it (see Phone assistants).

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Swimtime Pal shows timer controls on your lock screen while a swim runs and, if you turn them on, pool-day reminders. They are made on the phone; nothing is sent to us.

Microphone (optional)

Voice commands are off until you turn them on. Then the microphone listens only after you tap the mic button, for one short phrase, using your phone's own on-device speech recognition. Nothing is recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Health Connect (optional)

Health Connect is part of Pro. If you connect it, Swimtime Pal saves each swim there (time, distance, lengths), can read heart rate during your swims, and can bring in swims other apps saved there. This stays on your phone. You can disconnect any time in Settings or in Health Connect.

Location (open water, optional)

Swimtime Pal asks for your location only when you start an open-water swim, and only while the app is in use. During that swim it reads GPS about every 5 seconds to measure your distance and draw your path on screen; a notification shows while it does. When you finish, only the distance and time are saved, on this phone. The path is not kept and no location is ever sent to us.

Check-in text (optional)

If you add a check-in contact, their name and phone number are saved on this phone. Before a swim, Swimtime Pal can open your own Messages app with a text ready (where you are swimming and when you plan to be back). You send it yourself; Swimtime Pal never sends or reads texts.

Trainer mode (optional)

Trainer mode links your phone to a trainer's phone nearby, directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi (Google Nearby), with no internet and no accounts. You choose what the trainer sees (clock, reps, heart rate, notes); nothing else is shared, and the link ends with your swim. Nothing goes to us.

Your watch

With the Wear OS app, the phone and your watch share the running timer and your button presses over your own Bluetooth connection. Nothing goes to us.

Widget, shortcuts and phone assistants

The home-screen widget shows this week's distance and your next swim. "Hey Google", shortcuts and phone assistants (like Gemini, or other assistants on your phone that Android lets call apps) work with Swimtime Pal on the phone; we receive nothing. What an assistant may do is your choice in Settings > Privacy and data > Phone assistants. Start and pause only (the default): it can start, pause and open Swimtime Pal and open the builder or swim log for you to finish, and sees none of your swims. Read my summary: it can also read this week, month and year, your streak, your next workout and your last swim. Full access: it can also read your recent swims, best times and waiting workouts, and save a swim or build a workout for you. Swimtime Pal keeps that summary on this phone only while you allow it. Once an assistant has an answer, the assistant's own privacy policy applies, and it may send what you ask it to its maker's servers.

Deleting

Settings > Privacy and data > Delete all my data erases everything Swimtime Pal saved on this phone, including your own sounds and anything kept for phone assistants, and sets assistant access back to Start and pause only. Uninstalling the app also removes it. Swims already saved to Health Connect stay there until you delete them in Health Connect. The dates of your watched-ad extras are kept on purpose, so the once-a-day limit can't be reset (see Watched-ad extras).

Children

Swimtime Pal is not directed at children under 13.

Watched-ad extras (Free, optional)

If you choose to watch an ad for an extra (one more workout, a pace test, a Pro practice type or a Pro look for the day), the ad comes from Google AdMob as above. The day you last used each extra is saved on this phone and in Google Block Store, which keeps it with your Google account so it survives a reinstall. It holds only those dates, nothing about your swimming, and we never receive it. These dates stay after Delete all my data or a reinstall, so the once-a-day limit can't be reset; Google keeps them under your Google account's backup settings.

Phone assistants

You choose what phone assistants (like Gemini) may do in Swimtime Pal, in Settings > Privacy and data. With Full access, what an assistant saves or builds for you counts as if you did it, so check your logbook. We don't make or control phone assistants and are not responsible for what they do, mishear or share; their own terms and privacy policies apply. You can lower or turn off their access at any time.
