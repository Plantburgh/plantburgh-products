Swimtime Pal: Delete Your Data

Last updated: 2026-10-20 · Plantburgh Products LLC, a Pennsylvania company owned by Plantburgh LLC

Open Swimtime Pal, go to Settings › Privacy and data › Delete all my data, and confirm. That erases every workout, plan, personal best and setting on this phone. Uninstalling the app also removes it. Swims you saved to Health Connect stay there until you delete them in Health Connect. A Google Play subscription is not cancelled by deleting data; cancel it in Google Play.
