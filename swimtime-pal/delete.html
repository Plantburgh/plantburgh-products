Swimtime Pal: Delete Your Data

Last updated: November 22, 2026 · Plantburgh Products LLC, a Pennsylvania company owned by Plantburgh LLC

Open Swimtime Pal, go to Settings › Privacy and data › Delete all my data, and confirm. That erases every workout, plan, personal best and setting on this phone. Uninstalling the app also removes it. Swims you saved to Health Connect stay there until you delete them in Health Connect. It also clears what you let phone assistants read and sets their access back to Start and pause only. The dates of your watched-ad extras stay (on the phone and in Google Block Store, with your Google account) so the once-a-day limit can't be reset; they hold only dates, nothing about your swimming, and we never receive them. We hold none of your data on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete for you. A Google Play subscription is not cancelled by deleting data; cancel it in Google Play.
