The app

Flipline is a tool for tracking a personal reselling operation: sourcing, valuing, inventory, listing workflow, and bookkeeping. It runs on your device with your data.

No advice

Numbers in Flipline (value estimates, deal scores, margins, tax set-asides, Schedule-C-shaped exports) are informational tools, not financial, legal, or tax advice. Verify prices, item authenticity, and tax treatment yourself; consult a professional for tax filings.

Photo identification, safety and recall warnings, and resale checks can be wrong or incomplete. AI can misname an item, and a recall or can't-resell check only covers what its source lists. A missing warning does not mean an item is safe or legal to sell. Check before you buy, list or ship.

Deals in the feed come from other companies' sites and feeds. Prices, stock and end times can change before you get there; the seller's own page is what counts.

Your accounts and keys

Third-party connections (marketplace APIs, AI providers, deal sources) use credentials you create and own. You are responsible for those accounts, their fees, and complying with each provider's terms. Flipline only ever calls official APIs and public feeds, with your keys at your request, and never automates your marketplace accounts without an explicit action from you.

Your data, your responsibility

All data is stored locally on your device. Use the built-in backup; device loss without a backup means data loss. We have no copy.

Home-screen widgets show your recent profit and deals on the home screen. Place them only where you are happy for others to see those numbers.

Flipline Pro subscription

Flipline Pro is an optional subscription, monthly or yearly, sold through Google Play. The free app keeps working without it.

  • Billing. Google Play charges your Google account. The price, the billing period and any free trial are shown in the app and by Google Play before you confirm. Flipline never sees your card.
  • Free trial. If a plan offers one, it is shown before you buy. You are charged when the trial ends unless you cancel before then.
  • Renewal. Pro renews automatically at the end of each period until you cancel.
  • Cancelling. Cancel any time in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions, or with Manage subscription on your Profile in the app (More › Account & settings › Profile). You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for; it does not renew after that.
  • Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy. Request one through Google Play.
  • New phone or reinstall. Use Restore purchases on the Pro sheet, signed in to the same Google account.
  • What Pro includes is listed on the Pro sheet: every comp behind every price, more of your own deal feeds, 90 days of price history, posting to eBay in one tap, CSV exports of snaps and comps, My Stuff and inventory (backups and the tax CSVs stay free), no banner ads, and the option to hide sponsored offers. Some Pro features need accounts or keys of your own (for example, posting to eBay needs your own eBay developer keys and sign-in); those requirements are shown in the app.
