Flipline: Delete Your Data

Plantburgh Products LLC · updated 2026-11-22

Flipline has no accounts and no server that keeps your records. Everything you enter is stored on your phone, so you delete it on your phone. We hold no copy and have nothing to delete for you.

Steps

  1. Open Flipline.
  2. Go to More › Account & settings › Erase all data.
  3. Type ERASE and confirm.

Uninstalling the app also removes everything on the phone. Remove any Flipline home-screen widgets too.

What is deleted

  • Your inventory, purchases, sales, expenses, mileage, comps, hunts, feeds, lists, collections (with any place you noted) and profile.
  • Every photo and receipt the app saved.
  • Every API key and sign-in stored in the phone's key store (AI providers, deal sources, eBay).
  • The records copy kept for Android backup. Google replaces its stored backup at the phone's next backup; to remove it now, turn off backup for Flipline in your phone's Settings › Google › Backup.
  • Cached pictures and the file the home-screen widgets read.

What is not deleted, and who holds it

  • Photos and searches already sent to an AI provider or search service you chose are held by that provider under its own policy. Disconnect it in the app and revoke the key with that provider.
  • Ad data that Google's ads software sent to Google is Google's. Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings › Privacy › Ads.
  • A Flipline Pro subscription lives in Google Play and is not cancelled by erasing data. Cancel it in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.
