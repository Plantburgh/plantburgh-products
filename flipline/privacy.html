Flipline Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-07-26

Flipline is an offline-first reselling companion. This policy is short because the app is built not to collect your data.

What we collect

Nothing. Flipline has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no ads, no tracking SDKs, and no server operated by us. We (the developer) never see your data.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter — inventory, purchases, sales, expenses, mileage, photos, API keys — is stored in a local database on your device only. Backups exist only where you choose to share or save them.

What leaves your device

Data leaves your device only when you trigger an action that calls a third-party service, using credentials you supplied:

  • Deal sources and price lookups (e.g. GSA Auctions, Best Buy, Walmart, Woot, Discogs, Open Library, Open Food Facts, UPCitemdb): your search or refresh sends the query to that service.
  • Photo ID: when you snap a photo for identification, that photo is sent to the AI provider you connected (Google Gemini and/or Anthropic Claude) under your own API key and their terms. Photos are never sent automatically.
  • Sharing: backups and CSV exports go wherever you share them.

Each third-party service processes those requests under its own privacy policy. Flipline adds no identifiers of its own to any request.

Data retention and deletion

We retain nothing, so there is nothing for us to delete. To erase all app data, delete the app (or clear its storage). To revoke an AI provider's access, disconnect it in the app and revoke the key with that provider.

Children

Flipline is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If a future version ever changes what leaves the device, this policy and the in-app copy will be updated first, and the change will be opt-in.
