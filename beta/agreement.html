Tester agreement

Last updated: 2026-10-03

Who this is with

This agreement is between you and Plantburgh Products LLC, a Pennsylvania company owned by Plantburgh LLC ("we", "us"). It applies to every app you get as a tester. Each app's own terms and privacy policy apply too.

What you get

Free use of the tester apps during testing. Where an app has paid features, Andre gives you a code. Codes are personal, tied to your phone, can't be transferred, and end on the date shown with the code. We may renew them or not.

What testing means

The apps are unfinished. Features can break, change or disappear, and an app can be paused or stopped at any time. Don't rely on a tester app for anything important without keeping your own records.

Your data

The apps keep your data on your phone, not with us. Back up what matters using each app's export. An update or a reinstall can lose data, and we can't recover it.

Feedback

When you send feedback, you let us use it to improve our products without paying you or crediting you. Only send what you are comfortable sharing.

Keep it between us

Please don't post the install files, codes or unreleased features publicly, and don't pass the files on. Ask Andre if a friend wants to join.

Founder builds

Some apps also have a founder build used by Plantburgh for testing (for example "Duesy Personal" or "Drift Dev"). These are not part of the tester program and must not be shared.

Ending access

You can stop testing at any time by uninstalling the apps. We can end the program, or your access, at any time; your codes then stop working, and the data on your phone stays yours.

Age

You must be 18 or older to join the tester program.

No warranty; limits

The apps are provided "as is", without warranties. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for lost data or for indirect or consequential damages. Our total liability to you is limited to the minimum the law allows.

Changes and law

We may update this agreement; this page shows the date of the current version. Pennsylvania law applies.
