Plantburgh Plant Id: Delete Your Data

Last updated: 2026-11-10 · Plantburgh Products LLC, a Pennsylvania company owned by Plantburgh LLC

In the app

Open Settings › Delete account and confirm. That deletes your account and everything stored under it right away: garden, folders, notes, plant photos, reminders, wish list, scan and diagnosis history with their photos, Plant Advisor conversations, expert questions, usage counts and Google Play subscription links.

Without the app

Email contact@plantburgh.com with the subject Delete my Plant Id account. If you linked a Google account, send it from that address. If you use the app as a guest, include the account code from Settings › About. We delete the account and everything stored under it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is kept

  • A Google Play subscription is not cancelled by deleting your account. Cancel it in Google Play first, or it keeps renewing.
  • To give one free trial per phone, a one-way code made from your phone's ID is kept, without anything else about you.
  • Anonymous daily totals (for example, how many scans happened in a day) are kept; they cannot be linked to you.
