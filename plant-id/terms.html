Using Plant ID

Plantburgh Plant ID is made by Plantburgh Products LLC. By using the app you agree to these terms and to the privacy policy.

AI results can be wrong

Plant names, diagnoses, plant problem matches, repot checks, watering amounts, light levels, care notes and native or invasive status come from our plant model, our AI provider and public sources, and can be wrong. Look twice before you act on a result. If an answer is wrong, harmful or offensive, tap Report this answer under it (Plant Advisor and diagnosis) or use the menu on any section of a plant page; a person at Plantburgh reads every report.

Check with a professional

Plant ID is a guide, not a professional service. Before you act on a result, such as eating or foraging a plant, treating a person or a pet, spraying a chemical, pruning or removing a tree, or paying for a fix, check with a qualified professional (a doctor, a veterinarian, Poison Control, a certified arborist, your local extension office or a licensed pesticide applicator) and get a second opinion. What you do with a result is your decision.

Eating, foraging and toxicity

Never eat, forage or use a wild plant as food or medicine because of something the app says. Have an expert identify it in person first. Toxicity notes are general guidance. If a person or a pet has eaten a plant, call a doctor, a vet or poison control right away.

Plans and fair use

  • A photo scan is one identify, diagnose or repot check. The free plan and Premium each have a daily and a monthly number of scans and other allowances; Settings › Your plan shows yours and what is left. Days follow the UTC calendar.
  • An answer read from a plant label in your photo does not use a scan.
  • On the free plan you may be offered a short ad to watch for one extra scan. It is at most one a day for each account and each phone, it counts only when the ad is watched to the end, it is never offered during the free trial or with Premium, and it has no cash value.
  • Premium is for one person and their plants. Very heavy use can be slowed for a while: answers may come from a lighter AI model and scans may be limited. Normal use never sees it.

Free trial

The free trial gives Premium for a set number of days, shown in the app. It never asks for a card and is never billed. When it ends, your account is on the free plan. One trial per account and per phone.

Premium

Premium is a Google Play subscription, billed monthly. A yearly plan may be offered later. In the US it is $9.99 a month; the price in your country is shown in the app and Google Play before you buy. It renews until you cancel. To cancel, open Google Play, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › Plantburgh Plant ID › Cancel subscription. Premium stays until the end of the paid period. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel it. Refunds follow Google Play's policies. If we stop offering Premium, we will not charge for time you did not get.

Rules

Do not upload photos you have no right to share, automate scans or page views, copy the plant pages in bulk, resell results, use many accounts or phones to get around limits, or send abusive messages to Plant Advisor or to our experts. We can slow, block or close accounts that do.

Photos you choose to share

Sharing is off unless you turn it on in Settings. If you turn it on, you give us permission to show the photos of plants you save to My Garden to other people in the app, on that plant's page, with the name you chose or as "A Plantburgh Plant ID user". We look at each photo before it is shown and may decline any photo. Share only photos you took yourself, and no photos of people. You keep ownership of your photos. Turn sharing off, or delete your account, and we remove the photos you shared. Anyone can report a shared photo by opening it and tapping Report photo: it is taken down while we look at it again, and the person who reported it no longer sees photos from that sharer. Photos that show people, private information, or anything unlawful, hateful, sexual or violent are not allowed and are removed.

AI assistants

You can let an AI assistant you use (for example ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini) look things up in Plant ID, and, if you switch it on, read your garden and look at your plant photos. The assistant is another company's service under its own terms. What it tells you, including anything it says about a photo or a plant problem, is its answer, not ours: it can be wrong and it is not a diagnosis. Your personal address works like a key: keep it private, and turn it off or make a new one if you think someone else has it. Each address has a daily limit, and we can turn off an address that is misused.
