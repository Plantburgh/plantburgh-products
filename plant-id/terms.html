AI results

Names, diagnoses, repot checks, watering amounts and care notes come from an AI and can be wrong. Look twice before you eat, touch or treat anything based on a result.

Toxicity

Toxicity notes are general guidance from published sources. If a person or a pet has eaten a plant, call a doctor, a vet or poison control right away.

Fair use

A photo scan is one identify, diagnose or repot check. Free accounts get 5 scans a day and Premium gets 100, counted by the UTC day. Premium is for one person and their plants; an account that keeps hitting the cap may be slowed for a few days.

Premium

Premium is a monthly or yearly Google Play subscription that renews until you cancel in Google Play. Refunds follow Google Play's policies.

Rules

Do not upload photos you have no right to share, automate scans, resell results, or get around the daily limits. We can suspend accounts that do. If we retire Premium, we will not charge for time you did not get.
