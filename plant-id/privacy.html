Plantburgh Plant Id: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-20 · Plantburgh Products LLC, a Pennsylvania company owned by Plantburgh LLC

The short version: To identify or diagnose a plant, the photo you scan is sent to our server and our AI provider. Your garden, reminders and scan history are stored on our servers under a guest account tied to your phone. The free tier shows ads.

Who we are

Plantburgh Plant Id is made by Plantburgh Products LLC, a Pennsylvania company owned by Plantburgh LLC. Questions: contact@plantburgh.com.

Your account

The app signs you in as a guest tied to this phone, using Google Firebase. You can add an email address to keep your garden on a new phone. If you uninstall or clear the app's data without adding an email, a new guest starts and the old data can no longer be reached from the phone.

Photos you scan

Each photo you scan is sent to our server (Google Cloud, United States) and to our AI provider, Anthropic, to produce the identification, diagnosis or care answer. Anthropic processes it under its commercial API terms, which do not allow using it to train models. We keep your recent scans in your history so you can look back at them.

What we store

Your garden (plants, notes, care settings), reminders, scan history and photos, your plan and daily scan counts, stored in Google Firebase under your account.

Ads on the free tier

The free tier shows a Google AdMob banner and, when AdMob has nothing, one of our own products or a partner offer. Google may use your phone's advertising ID, IP address and device details to show and measure ads under Google's policies. Your photos and garden are never sent to advertisers. Premium shows no ads.

Purchases

Premium is billed by Google Play. Google tells our server which plan your account has. We never see your payment details.

Notifications

Care reminders are scheduled on the phone.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the services above that run the app (Google Firebase and Cloud, Anthropic, Google AdMob and Google Play) or when the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting

We keep your data until you delete your account. See the account deletion page linked at the bottom for how to delete your account and everything stored under it.

Children

Plantburgh Plant Id is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions: contact@plantburgh.com.
