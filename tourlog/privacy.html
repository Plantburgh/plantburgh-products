TourLog: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-21 · Plantburgh Products LLC, a Pennsylvania company owned by Plantburgh LLC

The short version: Recordings, photos, notes and transcripts stay on your phone. The transcript is made on the phone. There is no account and we run no server that holds anything of yours. The free plan shows ads; ads are never given your recordings or transcripts.

Who we are

TourLog is made by Plantburgh Products LLC, a Pennsylvania company owned by Plantburgh LLC. Questions: contact@plantburgh.com.

What the app keeps, and where

Your recordings, photos, marks, notes, follow-ups, voice names, transcripts, the names of people and places you type, and your settings are stored only on this phone, in the app's private storage. There is no account and we never receive them. They are not included in Google's cloud backup.

Microphone

The microphone is used only while you are recording a tour. A notification shows the whole time it is on. Nothing can start a recording except your tap.

Camera

Photos you take during a tour are saved inside the app on this phone.

Transcripts and follow-ups

Speech-to-text, voice labels and the list of what was promised are worked out on the phone. Audio and text are never uploaded for this.

When the app uses the internet

  • Transcript tools. Downloaded once from the open-source sherpa-onnx releases on GitHub.
  • The plan file. A small file from our website that holds the free plan's numbers, so they can change without an app update.
  • Google Play. To show prices and to buy, restore or check a TourLog Plus subscription. Google handles the payment; we never see your card.
  • Ads, on the free plan only. See below.

Each of these carries what any web request does (your IP address) and nothing about your recordings.

Ads on the free plan

If you are on the free plan and your trial is over, the Tours and Search screens show a small strip of ads. There are no ads during the trial or with Plus, and never on the recording screen, on a transcript, or on anything you send to someone.

  • Our own cards and partner offers. The list is fetched from our server (app.giganize.work). The request says only that it is TourLog asking. If you tap a card, the link goes through our server so we can count the tap, then opens the other site, which has its own privacy policy. Partner offers may earn us a commission.
  • Google AdMob. When it is switched on, Google may show ads in the strip. Google collects and uses device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), your IP address and how you interact with ads, as described in Google's privacy policy. Where the law requires it, Google's consent form is shown first, and you can change your choice later from the app.

Ads never receive your recordings, transcripts, photos, notes or the names you type.

Phone assistant (optional)

If you turn it on, an assistant on your phone can read the tours you ask it about: titles, transcripts, marks, notes and follow-ups. It cannot record, change or delete anything, and it never sees tours in the Trash. What it does with what it reads is covered by that assistant's own privacy policy.

Sharing and copies

Data leaves the app only when you share something or save a copy, and you choose where it goes. A copy saved to Downloads or another folder sits outside the app's private storage, where other apps you have allowed to read files can see it.

Recording other people

Many places, including Pennsylvania, require everyone in a conversation to agree before it is recorded. You are responsible for getting that consent. The app reminds you before each recording and notes the time you confirmed it.

Deleting

Delete a tour inside the app: it waits in the Trash for 30 days, then it is removed for good; you can empty the Trash sooner. "Delete everything on this phone" under You removes every tour and setting at once. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. Copies you saved elsewhere are yours to delete. See Delete your data.

Children

TourLog is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
