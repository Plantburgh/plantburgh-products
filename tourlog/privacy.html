TourLog: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-22 · Plantburgh Products LLC, a Pennsylvania company owned by Plantburgh LLC

The short version: Recordings, photos, notes and transcripts stay on your phone. The transcript is made on the phone. There is no account and we run no server that holds anything of yours. The free plan shows ads; ads are never given your recordings or transcripts.

Who we are

TourLog is made by Plantburgh Products LLC, a Pennsylvania company owned by Plantburgh LLC. Questions: contact@plantburgh.com.

What the app keeps, and where

Your recordings, photos, marks, notes, follow-ups, voice names, transcripts, the names of people and places you type, and your settings are stored only on this phone, in the app's private storage. There is no account and we never receive them. They are not included in Google's cloud backup.

One thing is kept outside the app: the day your free trial started, so the trial runs once per Google account. On Android it is stored with Google Play services' Block Store, which belongs to your Google account, outlasts uninstalling the app and is restored on a new phone with that account. If you watch an ad for an extra transcript, the count of extras for that month and the day you last earned one are kept there too, so a reinstall can't reset the limit. That is all that is kept there, and we never receive it.

Microphone

The microphone is used only while you are recording a tour. A notification shows the whole time it is on. Nothing can start a recording except your tap.

Camera

Photos you take during a tour are saved inside the app on this phone.

Transcripts and follow-ups

Speech-to-text, voice labels and the list of what was promised are worked out on the phone. Audio and text are never uploaded for this.

Your own voice (optional)

When you tap "This is me" on a tour, the app can remember your voice so it can name it on later tours without being asked. What it keeps is a short list of numbers that describes how your voice sounds, made on the phone and stored only on this phone. It is never uploaded, never put in a copy or anything you share, and is used for nothing else. Only the voice you mark as your own is remembered; other people's voices are never remembered from one tour to the next. Turn it off under You and it is forgotten at once; "Delete everything on this phone" and uninstalling the app remove it too.

Live words (optional, Plus)

If you switch on Live words, rough words appear on the recording screen while people talk. This also runs on the phone, with its own one-time download. No audio is sent anywhere, and the rough words are thrown away when the recording stops.

Space saver (optional)

If you switch it on, the audio of tours older than 30 days that already have a transcript is made smaller on the phone. Nothing is uploaded.

App lock (optional)

If you switch it on, TourLog opens only after your phone's own screen lock (fingerprint, face or PIN). The phone does the check; the app never sees your fingerprint, face or PIN.

When the app uses the internet

  • Transcript tools. Downloaded once from the open-source sherpa-onnx releases on GitHub (the Live words tool too, if you ask for it). Each file is checked before it is used.
  • The plan file. A small file from our website that holds the free plan's numbers, so they can change without an app update.
  • Google Play. To show prices and to buy, restore or check a TourLog Plus subscription. Google handles the payment; we never see your card.
  • Ads, on the free plan only. See below.

Each of these carries what any web request does (your IP address) and nothing about your recordings.

Ads on the free plan

If you are on the free plan and your trial is over, the Tours and Search screens show a small strip of ads. There are no ads during the trial or with Plus, and never on the recording screen, on a transcript, or on anything you send to someone.

  • Our own cards and partner offers. The list is fetched from our server (app.giganize.work). The request says only that it is TourLog asking. If you tap a card, the link goes through our server so we can count the tap, then opens the other site, which has its own privacy policy. Partner offers may earn us a commission.
  • Google AdMob. When it is switched on, Google may show ads in the strip. Google collects and uses device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), your IP address and how you interact with ads, as described in Google's privacy policy. Where the law requires it, Google's consent form is shown first, and you can change your choice later from the app.

Ads never receive your recordings, transcripts, photos, notes or the names you type.

Phone assistant (optional)

If you turn it on, an assistant on your phone can read the tours you ask it about: titles, transcripts, summaries, marks, notes and follow-ups. It only reads, unless you also turn on "Let assistants make changes": then it can rename and file tours, add notes and to-dos, tick them off, and move a tour to the Trash (where it stays 30 days). It can never record or delete anything for good, and it never sees tours in the Trash. Both are off until you turn them on. What an assistant does with what it reads is covered by that assistant's own privacy policy. This goes straight from TourLog to the assistant on your phone; we have no server in it.

AI summaries (optional)

Some phones have an AI built in (for example Gemini Nano on newer Android phones). On those phones, and only there, you can ask TourLog to write a short summary of an English tour. The phone's own AI writes it on the phone: the words are not sent to us or to anyone else. Google, which makes that AI, may receive general information about how the feature runs (not your words) under Google's privacy policy. A phone without it simply never shows the option. A summary can be wrong; check it against the words.

How many AI summaries and full summaries you used this month, and an extra earned by watching an ad, are counted on your phone only.

If you send a tour to an AI app you use (such as Muse, Gemini, ChatGPT or Claude) with Share, that app receives exactly what you send and handles it under its own privacy policy. TourLog never sends a tour to an AI service by itself.

Sharing and copies

Data leaves the app only when you share something or save a copy, and you choose where it goes. A copy saved to Downloads or another folder sits outside the app's private storage, where other apps you have allowed to read files can see it.

Safe copy (Plus, optional) copies each finished tour to a folder you pick, on the phone or in a cloud folder your phone offers, such as Google Drive. A cloud folder is run by its provider under that provider's privacy policy. We have no server in this and never see the copy.

Languages

The app's screens are available in English, Spanish, Chinese, Tagalog, Vietnamese and Arabic. The English version of this policy is the one that applies.

Recording other people

Many places, including Pennsylvania, require everyone in a conversation to agree before it is recorded. You are responsible for getting that consent. The app reminds you before each recording and notes the time you confirmed it.

Deleting

Delete a tour inside the app: it waits in the Trash for 30 days, then it is removed for good; you can empty the Trash sooner. "Delete everything on this phone" under You removes every tour and setting at once. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on the phone; only the day your free trial started and this month's count of watched-ad extras stay in your Google account, as described above. Copies you saved elsewhere are yours to delete. See Delete your data.

Children

TourLog is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
