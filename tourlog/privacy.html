TourLog: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-20 · Plantburgh Products LLC, a Pennsylvania company owned by Plantburgh LLC

The short version: Recordings, photos and transcripts stay on your phone. Transcription runs on the phone. There is no account, no ads and no server of ours.

Who we are

TourLog is made by Plantburgh Products LLC, a Pennsylvania company owned by Plantburgh LLC. Questions: contact@plantburgh.com.

What the app keeps

Your recordings, photos, bookmarks, notes, speaker names and transcripts, stored only on this phone in the app's private storage. There is no account and we never receive them.

Microphone

The microphone is used only while you are recording a tour. A notification shows the whole time it is on.

Camera

Photos you take during a tour are saved inside the app on this phone.

Transcription

Speech-to-text and speaker labels run on the phone. Audio is never uploaded for transcription.

Internet

The app connects to the internet only to download the speech models once (from the open-source sherpa-onnx releases on GitHub). That download carries what any web request does (your IP address) and nothing about your recordings.

Phone assistant (optional)

If you turn on the assistant feature, an assistant on your phone can read the transcripts you ask it about. It cannot record, change or delete anything. What it does with what it reads is covered by that assistant's own privacy policy.

Sharing

Data leaves the phone only when you share a transcript or save a file to Downloads, and you choose where it goes.

Recording other people

Many places, including Pennsylvania, require everyone in a conversation to agree before it is recorded. You are responsible for getting that consent. The app can show a consent notice before you start.

Deleting

Delete a tour, its recording or its photos inside the app. Speech models can be deleted in Settings. Uninstalling the app removes everything.

Children

TourLog is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions: contact@plantburgh.com.
