TourLog: Delete Your Data

Last updated: 2026-11-21 · Plantburgh Products LLC, a Pennsylvania company owned by Plantburgh LLC

TourLog has no account and we hold no copy of anything you record. Everything is on your phone, so you delete it there.

One tour

Open the tour, tap the three dots, then Move to Trash. It waits in the Trash for 30 days in case it was a mistake, then its recording, photos, notes and transcript are removed for good. To remove it sooner: You › Trash › Empty the Trash now.

Everything

You › Delete everything on this phone removes every tour and every setting at once and puts the app back to how it was when you installed it. Uninstalling the app does the same. Neither one starts a new free trial or resets the watched-ad extras: the day the trial started and this month's count of extras are kept in your Google account, and nothing else is.

Your remembered voice

If you let the app know your voice, turn off You › Know my voice on new tours and it is forgotten at once. Delete everything and uninstalling remove it too.

Copies you made

Files you saved to Downloads or another folder (including Safe copy's folder), or shared to another app, are outside TourLog. Delete them where you put them.

A subscription is separate

Deleting your data or uninstalling the app does not cancel TourLog Plus. Cancel it in Google Play › Payments and subscriptions, or from You › Your plan in the app.

Ads

If Google ads were shown to you on the free plan, what Google holds is covered by Google's privacy policy and can be managed in your Google account's ad settings.
