TourLog: Terms of Use

Last updated: 2026-11-21 · Plantburgh Products LLC, a Pennsylvania company owned by Plantburgh LLC

Consent to record

Only record people who have agreed to it, as the law where you are requires. In Pennsylvania and many other places, everyone in the conversation must agree. Getting that consent is your responsibility, not ours.

Transcripts are automatic

Transcripts, Live words, voice labels (including the voice the app names as yours), the list of what was promised, and prices picked out of a conversation are made by software and can be wrong or incomplete. Check anything important against the recording before you rely on it or send it to someone.

Free and Plus

Recording, marks, photos and replay are free. The free plan includes a set number of transcripts each month and shows a small strip of ads. TourLog Plus removes the transcript limit and the ads and unlocks the extra tools shown in the app before you buy. What is in Plus and the free limits can change over time; recordings and transcripts you already have stay yours either way.

Free trial

Each Google account gets one free trial of everything, with no ads, for the number of days shown in the app. No payment details are asked for and nothing is charged when it ends: the app simply goes to the free plan. The day the trial started is kept in your Google account, so uninstalling and reinstalling the app, clearing its storage or moving to a new phone does not start a second trial.

Subscription

Plus is sold through Google Play, monthly or yearly, at the price Google Play shows before you confirm. It renews automatically at the end of each period until you cancel. Cancel any time in Google Play › Payments and subscriptions, or from You › Your plan in the app; Plus stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.

Yearly gets cheaper the longer you stay

The yearly plan steps down for each full year you stay subscribed: the first-year price, then a lower second-year price, then the lowest price every year after (in the US: $29.99, $24.99, then $19.99). Google Play does not lower a price by itself, so the app tells you when the lower price is ready, starting about 60 days before your renewal, and you confirm it once in Google Play. It starts at your next renewal, never mid-year. If you do not confirm, you renew at the price you have. Pausing, or a payment Google Play recovers, keeps your step (the time away does not count). If you cancel and come back within 7 days you keep your step; after that you start again at the first-year price. The monthly plan has one price and no steps.

Ads and partner offers

The free plan shows ads: our own apps and services, partner offers, and ads served by Google AdMob. Partner cards and ads link to other companies' sites; their products, prices and terms are theirs, and we may earn money when you tap or buy. Plus removes ads.

Your recordings are yours to look after

Everything is stored on your phone. We keep no copy and cannot recover a recording if the phone is lost, reset or replaced, or if the app is uninstalled. Save a copy of anything you need to keep.

If you switch on Space saver, the audio of tours older than 30 days that already have a transcript is replaced on the phone by a smaller copy. The full-size original is not kept and cannot be brought back. Leave Space saver off if you need the original audio, or save a copy first.
