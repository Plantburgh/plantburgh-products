Plantburgh Light Meter: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-11 · Plantburgh Products LLC, a Pennsylvania company owned by Plantburgh LLC

The short version: Readings, spots, photos and projects stay on your phone. There is no account and no analytics. The free version shows ads from a small feed we run; Pro removes them. Location is optional and stays on the phone.

Who we are

Plantburgh Light Meter is made by Plantburgh Products LLC, a Pennsylvania company owned by Plantburgh LLC. Questions: contact@plantburgh.com.

What the app keeps

Your light readings, spots, spot photos, plant checks, client projects, care reminders, home-screen widget pictures and settings. They are stored only on this phone, in the app's private storage. There is no account and we never receive them.

Sensors

The light sensor gives the reading. The compass (the phone's motion and magnetic sensors) tells which way a window faces. Both are read on the phone only and nothing is sent anywhere.

Location (optional)

The app asks once whether it may use your approximate location to put the real sun in the window picture. If you say no, it guesses from the phone's time zone instead. If you turn on Save spot location, new spots keep the town and ZIP where they were taken; the phone's own Android address lookup turns the approximate position into a town and ZIP code. The app asks only for approximate location, never your precise position. Your location is kept only on this phone, never sent to us, and never tracked in the background.

Camera and photos

Spot photos you take or pick are saved inside the app on this phone.

Internet

The app downloads two small files: the list of ad cards (from app.giganize.work, run by us) and the Pro plan list (from plantburgh.github.io), plus the pictures for the cards. These requests carry only what any web request does, such as your IP address, and nothing about your readings. Links to our website, Play or these pages open in your browser.

Ads

The free version shows one small card at a time: our own products, or a partner's product through an affiliate network (for example CJ). No ad network runs inside the app, there is no advertising ID, and nothing about you is used to pick the ads. Tapping a card opens it in your browser through our link counter, which counts taps per card per day and keeps no identifier. If you buy something, the partner's site and the affiliate network handle it under their own policies. Pro shows no ads.

Pro subscription and purchases

Pro is a Google Play subscription. Google Play takes the payment and tells the app whether Pro is active; we never see your payment details. Google may collect diagnostics about purchases under Google's own privacy policy.

Notifications

Watering and re-measure reminders are scheduled on the phone. Nothing is sent to us.

Home-screen widgets

The widgets show copies of your spots and reminders that the app keeps on this phone. A Quick read tap reads the light sensor on the phone.

Phone assistant (optional)

Android assistants can ask the app for a reading or a plant check through Android's app functions. What the assistant does with the answer is covered by that assistant's own privacy policy.

Sharing and backups

Data leaves the phone only when you share a PDF report, export a file or save a backup, and you choose where it goes. If Android backup is on, Android may include the app's data in your own Google account backup.

Deleting

Settings › Delete all my data erases everything the app saved on this phone. Uninstalling the app also removes it. We hold no copy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If the app starts handling data differently, this page is updated before that version is released, with the date at the top.

Contact

Questions: contact@plantburgh.com.
