Plantburgh Light Meter: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-20 · Plantburgh Products LLC, a Pennsylvania company owned by Plantburgh LLC

The short version: Readings, spots, photos and projects stay on your phone. There is no account, the app makes no internet requests of its own, and it shows no third-party ads.

Who we are

Plantburgh Light Meter is made by Plantburgh Products LLC, a Pennsylvania company owned by Plantburgh LLC. Questions: contact@plantburgh.com.

What the app keeps

Your light readings, spots, spot photos, plant checks, client projects, care reminders and settings. They are stored only on this phone, in the app's private storage. There is no account and we never receive them.

Location (optional)

If you allow location, the app uses it only when you add a spot or a project, to fill in the address on the phone. Your location is not sent to us or tracked in the background.

Camera and photos

Spot photos you take or pick are saved inside the app on this phone.

Internet

The app does not connect to the internet on its own. Links to our website, Play or the terms open in your browser.

Ads and purchases

This version shows only our own offline cards and no third-party ads. If a future version adds ads or a paid upgrade, this page will be updated before that version is released. Any purchase is handled by Google Play; we never see your payment details.

Notifications

Watering and care reminders are scheduled on the phone. Nothing is sent to us.

Phone assistant (optional)

If you turn on the assistant feature, an assistant on your phone can read readings you ask it about. What it does with them is covered by that assistant's own privacy policy.

Sharing and backups

Data leaves the phone only when you share a PDF report, export a file or save a backup, and you choose where it goes. If Android backup is on, Android may include the app's data in your own Google account backup.

Deleting

Settings › Delete all my data erases everything the app saved on this phone. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions: contact@plantburgh.com.
