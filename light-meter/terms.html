Plantburgh Light Meter: Terms of Use

Last updated: 2026-11-11 · Plantburgh Products LLC, a Pennsylvania company owned by Plantburgh LLC

Readings are estimates

Phone light sensors vary by model and are not lab instruments. Readings, sun times, the compass and plant suggestions are guidance, not a guarantee of how a plant will do.

Plant safety

Pet and toxicity notes are general guidance from published sources. If a person or pet eats a plant, call a doctor, a vet or poison control.

Free and Pro

The app is free to use with ads and some limits (for example how many spots you can save). Pro removes ads and unlocks the extra tools and limits shown in the app before you buy. What is in Pro and the free limits can change over time; anything you already saved stays yours.

Subscription

Pro is sold through Google Play, monthly or yearly, at the price Google Play shows before you confirm. It renews automatically at the end of each period until you cancel. Cancel any time in Google Play › Payments and subscriptions, or from Settings in the app; Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.

Yearly gets cheaper the longer you stay

The yearly plan steps down for each full year you stay subscribed: the first-year price, then a lower second-year price, then the lowest price every year after (in the US: $29.99, $24.99, then $19.99). The app offers the lower price when you've earned it; you switch with one tap and it starts at your next renewal, never mid-year. Pausing, or a payment Google Play recovers, keeps your step (the time away doesn't count). If you cancel and come back within 7 days you keep your step; after that you start again at the first-year price. The monthly plan has one price and no steps.
