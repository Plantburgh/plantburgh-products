The call moment

Know before you pick up

When someone on your ledger calls, a card sits between their number and the answer button: what they owe you (or you owe them), the top three reasons, and what's overdue. Use your phone while it rings and it shrinks to a slim strip. Matching happens on your phone, nothing is uploaded, and no call is ever blocked.

Both ways

Money, favors and things

Track what you lent and what you borrowed: cash, a ride, a drill, a swap of dinner for gas. Every row says which way it goes in plain words, so "you owe" never hides among "owes you".

Lists

Roommates, trips, teams

Group people into lists and see the whole house or the whole trip at a glance. Split a bill across a list in one go.

Honest limits

Lend on your own terms

Set a limit per person and a cap on everything out. Duesy warns at the moment you're about to lend past them, and never blocks unless you tell it to.

Kept accurate

A ledger that keeps up

Log payments from a home-screen widget, a shared Venmo screenshot, or a quick "did it go through?" check when you come back from a payment app. A weekly Tally-Up shows what's due, and stale balances get a gentle nudge to confirm.

Privacy mode

Amounts behind ••••

One tap hides every amount on screen, on widgets and on the call card; tap the eye to peek. Hide Duesy from recent apps, block screenshots, keep names off notifications, and lock the app with your fingerprint.

Private by design

No account. No server.

Everything is encrypted on your device. Your automatic backup is encrypted with a key we never have; updates to friends travel directly, signed, through apps you already use.

Your language

Six languages

English, Español, 中文, Tagalog, Tiếng Việt and العربية, following your phone's language, with right-to-left done properly.
