The call moment

Know before you pick up

Make Duesy your call-screening app and an incoming call shows your own note for that person — balance, due date, reliability — as a notification or a card over the dialer. Matching happens on your phone; nothing is uploaded, and no call is ever blocked.

Honest limits

Lend on your own terms

Set a limit per person and a cap on everything out. Duesy warns at the exact moment you're about to lend past them — and never blocks unless you tell it to.

Kept accurate

A ledger that keeps up

Log payments from the home widget, a shared Venmo screenshot, or a quick "did it go through?" check when you come back from a payment app. Stale balances get a gentle nudge to confirm or fix.

Private by design

No account. No server.

Everything is encrypted on your device. Backups need a passphrase only you know; updates to friends travel directly, signed, through apps you already use. On the lock screen, amounts stay hidden until you unlock.
