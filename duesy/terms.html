What Duesy is

Duesy is a private, on-device ledger for informal debts between you and people you know. It is a record-keeping tool: it does not move money, does not create legally enforceable obligations, and is not a lender, bank, or payment service. Payment deep-links (e.g. Cash App, Venmo) simply open third-party apps you already use, under their own terms.

Calls

Call features only annotate: Duesy shows your own notes for a caller and never blocks, screens out, or rates any call. If you make Duesy your call-screening app, your previous screening app stops screening; you can hand the role back at any time, or choose "After calls only" in Settings, Calls, which leaves your screening app in place.

Free, trial and subscription

Duesy is free to use: adding, settling and swapping debts, search, automatic backup and both languages never require payment. The free version shows a small banner ad. New installs get 30 days of Duesy Pro free. Pro ($2.99/month or $19.99/year, or a one-time price when offered) removes ads and adds the call moment, signed statements, payment plans, recurring debts, photos on debts, importing and the home-screen widget. Subscriptions are billed and managed by Google Play or the App Store and renew until cancelled there. If Pro lapses, everything you made with it stays readable.

Your data, your responsibility

Everything lives on your device. Automatic backup (on by default) keeps an encrypted copy with your phone's Google backup, but it depends on your Google account, a screen lock and Android's backup being on. You remain responsible for your records: exporting a backup (Settings, Backup) and remembering its passphrase is the fallback, and without that passphrase an exported backup cannot be decrypted by anyone, including us. Deleting the app deletes your ledger. Figures in Duesy are what you (or a friend's signed update) recorded; they are your records, not a statement of legal debt.

Acceptable use

Use Duesy only to track genuine informal arrangements between consenting people. Don't use it to harass anyone or to record unlawful transactions. Nudge messages and statements are drafts you review and send yourself; you are responsible for what you send.
