What Duesy is

Duesy is a private, on-device ledger for informal debts between you and people you know. It is a record-keeping tool: it does not move money, does not create legally enforceable obligations, and is not a lender, bank, or payment service. Payment deep-links (e.g. Cash App, Venmo) simply open third-party apps you already use, under their own terms.

You must be 18 or older to use Duesy.

Not a party to your debts

The debts, favors, loans and swaps you record are between you and the other people involved. Plantburgh Products LLC is not a party to any of them: we don't check that they are real or accurate, we don't lend, guarantee, collect or enforce anything, and we are not responsible for any amount anyone owes, pays, fails to pay, or disputes. Duesy is not financial, tax, credit or legal advice, and it does not report to credit bureaus. If you want to recover a debt, follow the laws where you and the other person live; Duesy is not a debt-collection service.

Calls

Call features only annotate: Duesy shows your own notes for a caller and never blocks, screens out, or rates any call. If you make Duesy your call-screening app, your previous screening app stops screening; you can hand the role back at any time, or choose “After calls only” in Settings, Calls, which leaves your screening app in place.

If you allow Duesy to display over other apps, it can show a card about the caller over the ringing screen, or a slim strip when you are using your phone. You can swipe it away; it closes when the call is answered, declined or ends, and never answers, declines or records a call.

Free, trial and subscription

Duesy is free to use within the free plan: up to 10 people, 25 open debts and 5 things lent or borrowed at a time. Settled debts and archived people don't count. Adding, settling and swapping debts, search, automatic backup, app lock, hiding amounts and every language are part of it. When the free plan is full you can settle or remove something, watch a short video ad for one extra place that lasts 7 days (one at a time of each kind, at most once a day, and only when one extra is enough), or subscribe to Pro. Nothing you already recorded is ever locked or hidden. The free version shows small ads: Google's banner and two small cards at the bottom, and a few cards between sections, for our own apps and services or for partner shops (a partner card says Sponsored, and we may earn a commission if you buy). One small card also sits at the foot of the Pro plans page and of the free-plan-full screen, below the choices. You get 30 days of Duesy Pro free, once per Google account, no card needed. Reinstalling the app or deleting your data does not start the 30 days again. After that, Pro is $4.99 a week, $9.99 a month, or a yearly subscription: $59.99 for the first year, then $5 less each year you stay subscribed, down to $29.99 from the seventh year, where it stays. Each drop needs your confirmation: from 60 days before your renewal Duesy shows the lower price and you confirm it in Google Play; it then applies from that renewal. If you confirm later, it applies from the renewal after you confirm. If you cancel, your price starts again at $59.99, unless you resubscribe within 7 days. Pausing, or a card problem fixed during Google Play's grace period, keeps your price; time paused doesn't count toward the next drop. Prices shown are US prices; Google Play sets the local price in other countries. Pro removes ads and the free plan's limits and adds the call moment (the card and alerts when someone on your ledger calls), signed statements, payment plans, recurring debts, photos on debts and swaps, importing, more than one people list, the five extra looks, the larger home-screen widgets (the quick-add and balance widgets are free) and the extra privacy tools: blocking screenshots, hiding Duesy in recent apps, quiet notifications, the dead man's switch, Scramble now and hiding amounts on widgets only. Delete all my Duesy data is always free. Subscriptions are billed and managed by Google Play and renew until cancelled there. If Pro lapses, everything you made with it stays readable.
